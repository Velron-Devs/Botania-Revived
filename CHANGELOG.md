# Changelog

## 2.0.1 (2026-11-10)

- Fixed the Ponder scenes of the generating and functional flowers, the Mana Spreader tiers and the Mana Lenses showing only the base platform: Ponder discarded every block placed above the floor because the scene structures only contained the floor layer. Those scenes now show their blocks. With the Ponder bundled in Create: Adapted, the scene texts now follow the game language instead of always showing English.
- Botania's own shaders are back: the Mana Pool water, the Pylons, the Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate and Mana Enchanter runes, the halos (Flugel Tiara, Babylon, Luminizer), the Gaia Guardian grain and deformation, its boss bar and the corrupted particles. If one of them fails to compile it falls back to the vanilla look and writes one line in the log.
- The Garden of Glass sky (planets, aurora, daily rainbow, colored stars) and the Magic Skies module sky (stars, planets and rainbow, also in the End) are back, together with the Starfield Creator block effect.
- The Lexica Botania is drawn as a 3D book in first person again, the Gaia Head shows your skin (or the skin of the mob you are watching from) as a block, as an item and worn, and a renamed Tiny Potato uses its named model.
- Create integration: fixed the Mana Motor item model failing to load with Create: Adapted. Create: Adapted needs the official Create jar in the `create_original` folder of the game directory.
- New optional integrations: Ears (armor with Phantom Ink no longer hides the Ears traits and the Flugel Tiara hides the Ears wings), Inventory Sorter (the Flower Pouch is not sorted) and Omni Pots (Botania soils work in the Omni Pot and the Infused Grass speeds it up with mana from a nearby pool, as a stand-in until Botany Pots is available).
- Masterful Botany 1.0.1 for 26.1.2 is the version the AlfSteel Spreader is tested against.
- Still not ported to 26.1.2: Ars Nouveau, Spectrum, Botany Pots, Quark and the Magic Skies sky in the Nether and in dimensions without sky.

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-10)

- New Mana Spreader tiers: the Elementium Spreader and the Terrasteel Spreader sit between the Elven and the Gaia Spreader (Elementium: 320 mana per burst, 2400 capacity; Terrasteel: 480 per burst, 4000 capacity). With Masterful Botany installed there is also the AlfSteel Spreader, made from a Gaia Spreader, 2 Alfsteel Ingots and a Dragonstone: it moves mana 4 times faster than the Gaia Spreader (`integrations.masterful_botany.alfsteel_spreader_speed_multiplier`, 4.0 by default). It does not exist without Masterful Botany.
- New Ring of the Aesir: craft a Ring of Thor, a Ring of Odin and a Ring of Loki together (same owner, or all unbound) to get one relic that gives the effects of the three at once. Tossing it with Shift pressed splits it back into the three rings.
- Create integration (only with Create: Adapted for Minecraft 26.1.2 installed): the new Mana Motor produces rotation from mana. Its speed is set with the value panel, it only spends mana while the network uses its stress, and it stops when it runs out. Speed, stress and mana cost are configurable under `integrations.create.mana_motor`. Create: Adapted needs the official Create jar in the `create_original` folder of the game directory.
- Thaumaturge integration (only with Thaumaturge installed): an aura node within 8 blocks makes generating and functional flowers, mana spreaders, mana sparks, the Botanical Brewery, the Runic Altar, the Mana Enchanter and the Elven Gateway work 20 times faster. The multiplier, the range and each of the affected blocks can be changed or turned off under `integrations.thaumaturge.node_boost`.
- Sophisticated Backpacks and Storage: new Petal Apothecary Upgrade (automates the Petal Apothecary, using a bucket of water per craft) and new Remote Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate Upgrade (works as a Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate and needs a Mana Spark in its own slot). The Runic Altar Upgrade now needs a Wand of the Forest in its own slot and the Elven Gateway Upgrade needs two Natura Pylons in its own slots. The recipes of the Mana Pile, Botanical Brewery and Runic Altar upgrades changed. A creative Mana Tablet now fills the Mana Pile of a backpack or chest. The Mana Pile base capacity is now 1,000,000 and old configs that still had 500,000 are updated once.
- The Asgardandelion is no longer made in the Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate: it is crafted in the Extreme Crafting Table of Re-Avaritia (and in a grid of Mechanical Crafters when Create is installed) with Infinity Ingots, an Infinity Catalyst, Neutronium Ingots and Neutronium Nuggets laid out like the flower.
- All settings now live in `botania-common.toml`: the Sophisticated, Create, Masterful Botany and Thaumaturge options are sections of it (`integrations.*`). The old `botania-sophisticated-common.toml` file is no longer read; copy any changed values into the new sections.
- New balance options in `botania-common.toml`, all on by default with new values (set them back to turn them off): Fabulous Mana Pool capacity (100,000,000), mana sparks moving mana in batches, cheaper and smaller-range Orechid and Orechid Ignem (the Ignem now works in any dimension), a slower and more expensive Marimorphosis, the Entropinnyum accepting extra mana, a Gaia Guardian damage cap of 10,000 and a reduced update frequency for Hopperhock and redstone checks of flowers. Runic Altar recipes can set `return_runes` to false to consume the runes.
- Every item with a mana bar now shows its mana as a number (for example 10/1000000) above the bar, inside the tooltip.
- Ponder: every generating and functional flower, every Mana Spreader tier, every Mana Lens, the Mana Motor and the AlfSteel Spreader have their own scene that explains what they do and shows them working. With Create installed, Ponder comes from Create itself.
- Lexicon: new Modules category with the Sophisticated, Asgard, Creative, Exploration, Magic Skies and Thaumaturge entries, and new pages for the new Mana Spreaders and the Ring of the Aesir. The Asgardandelion entry is no longer locked behind the Elven Lexicon.

## 1.0.7 (2026-11-09)

- Port of Botania Revived to Minecraft 26.1.2 and NeoForge 26.1.2 (Java 25).
- Mana Pile Upgrade for Sophisticated Backpacks, Storage and chests: a Load/Unload mode button (Unload charges your Botania items from the pile when a Mana Support Upgrade is in the same backpack, wherever the backpack is carried), automatic mana infusion with its own input, output and optional catalyst slots (it refills the input from the storage and puts the results back in it; recipes without a catalyst work with the catalyst slot empty), a base capacity of 1,000,000 like a Mana Pool, and a tall mana bar in the right column of the window that reserves its own columns and no longer overlaps the Tank.
- The Runic Altar, Elven Gateway and Botanical Brewery upgrades now craft automatically from the storage and put the results back in it. Their recipes now use a Glimmering Livingwood Log on top and an Elven Gateway Core at the bottom of the middle column.
- The Mana Pile Upgrade shows up as a workstation of Mana Infusion in JEI and REI, and the "+" button transfers an infusion recipe to its input and catalyst slots.
- Fractal creative sub tabs, Curios 15, Sodium, KubeJS 8, JEI, REI, Reliable Recipe Viewer (replaces EMI), Jade, WTHIT and Create Ponder (only when Create is installed) are supported again. Ponder has one scene per flower and shows the flower you pick.
- Thaumaturge 1.1.0: revealing helmets and the new Botanurgist's Inkwell, a scribing tool that refills with mana. Re-Avaritia: the Asgardandelion and the creative items can be made in its Extreme Crafting Table.
- Fixed the mana spreader and everything that depends on mana bursts (the Runic Altar and the Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate among them): the bursts got stuck inside the spreader because of a change in how projectiles move.
- Not ported yet: Spectrum, Botany Pots, Ars Nouveau and Inventory Sorter (no 26.1 versions), the custom shaders (Gaia Guardian grain, halo, pylons, pool water, starfield) and the Garden of Glass and Magic Skies skies.

## 1.0.6 (2026-11-05, Minecraft 1.21.1)

- Botania backpacks, barrels and chests (Manasteel, Elementium, Terrasteel, Gaia and Alfsteel) now open a wide inventory screen with 18 slot columns instead of 12, so they need far fewer rows and much less scrolling (695 slots take 39 rows instead of 58); the upgrade tabs stay on the left, the search and sort buttons on top, the scroll bar on the right and your inventory is centered below. It is purely visual and only active when Sophisticated Core is installed; other storages keep the normal screen. The panel backgrounds are based on Sophisticated ATM Tiers by GrafKakashi (MIT).
- Fixed the look of the Botania Barrel and Chest tiers (Manasteel, Elementium, Terrasteel, Gaia and Alfsteel): items and placed blocks now always carry an explicit wood type (also when obtained with `/give`, crafting or loot) instead of rendering with a stray pink body, the tier upgrade keeps the wood, paint and materials of the original, and the names of the barrels and chests no longer show broken text. The creative tab and the JEI/REI lists now show every variant (all wood types and the flat top barrel) of each tier like Sophisticated Storage does for its own tiers, instead of a single entry.

## 1.0.5 (2026-11-02)

- Fixed the recipe ids of the JEI categories showing as `Unknown` after running `/reload` (issue #11).

## 1.0.4 (2026-10-30)

- New Rune of Deception (`botania:rune_deception`). It is made in the Runic Altar from a Rune of Spring, a Rune of Water, Pixie Dust, a Dreamwood Twig and a Block of Mana Diamond for 20000 mana, has its own Lexicon page, counts as a rune for catalysts and advancements, and is registered like any other rune (KubeJS and `RegisterRunes` keep working).
- The Wither Mana Rose Runic Altar recipe now takes one of each ingredient: Unactivated Wither Mana Rose, Mana Diamond, Mana Pearl, Mana Powder, Loonium, Daybloom Motif, Rune of Pride, Rune of Deception, Rune of Fire and Rune of Water, and its mana cost was lowered from 100000 to 20000 so it crafts much faster.
- The Creative Crafting module id is now `creative` (config key `modules.creative`, recipes under `botania:creative/`) instead of `cre`, and it no longer adds its own creative tab, since the objects it covers are regular Botania items already listed in the Botania tab.
- Fixed tooltips of rings, relics and other items showing literal `&6`, `&7`, `&b` color codes in the Spanish and Dutch translations.
- New optional module `sophisticated` (`modules.sophisticated`, on by default; it only loads when Sophisticated Core and Sophisticated Backpacks or Sophisticated Storage are installed, otherwise it does nothing): four Botania Stack Upgrades (Manasteel x1M, Elementium x2M, Terrasteel x3M and Gaia x12B, crafted in a chain from an Upgrade Base; the limit of a slot is still capped at 2,147,483,647 items, so the Mana Pile Upgrade reaches its maximum capacity with any of them), the Mana Pile Upgrade, which stores mana in a backpack or storage (capacity grows with Stack Upgrades; it fills from Mana Tablets and Mirrors kept inside, in its own slot or carried in your inventory, and from Mana Spreaders and Sparks when the backpack, barrel or chest is placed as a block; it shows a vertical mana bar, has its own tab and can be switched off with the slot button), and the Mana Support Upgrade (also switchable), which lets the Botania items you carry spend the mana of the Mana Pile Upgrades of your backpacks. Capacity, absorption rate and switches are in `botania-sophisticated-common.toml`.
- The `sophisticated` module also adds four Botania Backpack tiers that continue the Sophisticated Backpacks chain after the Netherite Backpack: Manasteel (280 slots, 8 upgrade slots), Elementium (380/9), Terrasteel (480/10) and Gaia (595/10), each crafted from the previous backpack with four ingots (Gaia Ingots for the last one), keeping its contents, upgrades and colors, fire resistant, wearable on the back slot, and with slot counts and an on/off switch (`backpacks.enabled`) in the config.
- The `sophisticated` module also adds four Botania Barrel tiers and four Botania Chest tiers for Sophisticated Storage that continue its chain after the Netherite ones: Manasteel (280 slots, 6 upgrade slots), Elementium (380/7), Terrasteel (480/8) and Gaia (595/10). Each is upgraded in the crafting grid with eight ingots around the previous barrel or chest (double chests with the previous double chest and two ingots), keeps its contents, paint and upgrades, Terrasteel and Gaia are fire and blast resistant, and slots can be changed or the whole feature switched off in `botania-sophisticated-common.toml` (`storage` section). Four Tier Upgrade items (Netherite to Manasteel, Manasteel to Elementium, Elementium to Terrasteel and Terrasteel to Gaia, each crafted from eight ingots around a lever) upgrade a barrel or chest already placed in the world with a right click, keeping its contents, upgrades, paint, name and orientation (a double chest uses two items and both halves are upgraded). They only exist when Sophisticated Storage is installed. Storages with many slots (up to 695) use the scrollable inventory screen of Sophisticated Core, and a small client fix caps its height so the background draws correctly on tall screens.
- The `sophisticated` module also integrates with two optional mods. With Sophisticated ATM Tiers installed the chain starts from its Unobtainium backpack, barrel and chest instead of the Netherite ones (the Netherite to Manasteel Tier Upgrade item accepts both), and with Masterful Botany installed a fifth tier, Alfsteel (695 inventory slots, 10 upgrade slots, a practically infinite Alfsteel Stack Upgrade and a Gaia to Alfsteel Tier Upgrade item), continues the chain after Gaia.
- The `sophisticated` module also adds three crafting upgrades that run Botania recipes inside a backpack or storage, each with its own tab and slots and powered by the Mana Pile Upgrade of the same storage: the Runic Altar Upgrade (up to 16 ingredients plus a Livingrock, returns the runes, mana cost of the recipe), the Elven Portal Upgrade (trades the items in its slots automatically, 500 mana per trade) and the Botanical Brewery Upgrade (a vial, flask, incense stick or blood pendant plus up to 6 reagents). They wait while the storage has no Mana Pile Upgrade, lacks mana or has no room for the result. Switches, rates and costs are in `botania-sophisticated-common.toml` (`runic_altar`, `elven_portal`, `brewery`). With JEI or REI, the + button of the Runic Altar, Elven Trade and Brewery recipes works while one of these upgrades is installed in the open backpack or storage: it moves the ingredients from your inventory and the storage into the upgrade slots (hold Shift to fill the Elven Portal Upgrade with as many trades as you have), highlighting the missing ones.
- The Botania creative tab now has side sections that split its items by purpose (Generating, Functional, Mana Network, Gear, Accessories, Ingredients, Brewing, Decoration and Misc, plus one section for each active module with content: Botania Extras, Asgard and Sophisticated). The search bar still looks through all of them and items that other mods or KubeJS add to the Botania tab appear in Misc. The Asgard and Botania Extras modules no longer register their own creative tabs, and the selected section is client-only and is not remembered between sessions. The sections come from the Fractal library (MIT), bundled inside the mod.
